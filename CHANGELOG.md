# Memories changelog

## v0.7.2

- The animated background is now drawn directly on the screen at full sharpness instead of a small picture stretched up, so it is smooth and no longer looks pixelated; the grain over it is much subtler, and long tab names (like Feedback) shrink to fit instead of being cut off
- A Sort button on the Photos toolbar, so the sort options are easy to find
- Favouriting or deleting a photo opened from Wayfinder no longer crashes; it is matched to your library, and you are told if it isn't in it
- Back from a photo or video opened from another app closes Memories completely and returns you to where you were
- A smoother scroll handle that no longer jumps around
- Pictures from a computer (Ripple) are read once instead of three times, show a real progress bar with speed and time left, and appear rough-to-sharp in quick passes

## v0.7.1

- Screen recorder: the capture prompt is now limited to the entire screen (Android's default "A single app" mode records only one window and leaves everything else black), and recordings are tagged with standard video colours, so they no longer come out far too dark

## v0.7.0

- Cinema is now part of Memories. The Videos tab is Cinema's full video library and player (resume, gestures, subtitles, speed, picture-in-picture, network streams, casting, the editor), the Record tab is the screen recorder with clip mode, and everything Memories did before is unchanged
- The Launcher's floating button, the recording tile and the notification all control the recorder here now, and your videos and recordings stay where they were
- Opening a video from another app plays it in the full player; opening a photo or video from a folder in Wayfinder or on a computer shows that whole folder to swipe through, with the file name and position at the top
- Cinema's settings are in Memories' Settings under Playback, Touch controls, Subtitles, Editing and Library

## v0.6.2

- At night the animated background gets a stronger wash, so text stays easy to read on dark scenes

## v0.6.1

- Each app's animated scene now has its own drifting symbols (maths signs in Abacus, notes in Echo, arrows in Wayfinder and so on), and the scene is a little clearer while text stays easy to read
- Animated backgrounds reworked after your report: they are drawn at a higher resolution and smoothly at 24 frames a second without slowing the app down, and no longer show horizontal lines

## v0.6.0

- Opens a whole folder from Wayfinder to swipe through, with Back returning to it
- Every app window now has the animated scene behind it, in the app's own colour (for example orange water in Abacus, green in Pulse, purple in Scribble), following your Aqua, Bliss or Super look and the real sunrise and sunset. Motion Reduced shows a still picture and Off turns it off
- Help buttons (?) explain the more confusing settings

## v0.5.19

- Fixed a gap above the keyboard in screens with a bottom tab bar: the tab bar now steps aside while you type, so you can see what you are writing

## v0.5.18

- Fixed tapping a playing video never bringing the controls back (the tap used the controls state from when the video started)
- Fixed Memories reopening on a photo you had just deleted: the deleted photo is now removed from the open viewer's list as well

## v0.5.17

- Studio remembers your brush size, reach and colour

## v0.5.16

- New setting: how long a slideshow shows each photo

## v0.5.15

- Studio, the layered editor with magic wand, lasso, cut-outs, transparency, text, shapes and blend modes, now has its own button in the photo viewer instead of hiding inside Edit

## v0.5.14

- New settings: photos per row, keep the screen on while viewing, and Studio remembers PNG or JPEG
- Text size and accent colour chosen in Control Room now apply here
- New Rotate with the phone switch in Settings. Rotates by default

## v0.5.13

- Studio now has a lasso, paint bucket, shapes, colour picker, grow and shrink selection, blend modes, merge down and 24 steps of undo
- Studio Adjust: brightness, contrast, colour, warmth, black and white, sepia, invert and blur on a layer or just the selected part
- The Studio undo button now lights up as soon as there is something to undo, and the tool panel scrolls so the picture stays visible

## v0.5.12

- New Layers editor: magic wand, box select, brush, eraser that leaves see-through areas, cut a part out onto its own layer, move, resize and turn layers, add photos and text, save as PNG with transparency
- Replace the original from either editor, with a question first (saving a copy is still the default)
- Loading screens now say what they are doing

## v0.5.11

- My albums: make your own albums from any photos (select photos › Add to an album); one photo can be in as many albums as you like. Rename or delete an album from its ⋮ menu; the photos themselves are never touched
- Import albums from Google Photos: in the Albums tab ⋮ menu, pick the Takeout .zip you downloaded from Google and Memories makes an album for each of yours, matching photos already on this phone by file name

## v0.5.10

- On this day can be switched off: Settings › Photos, or Hide On this day in the ⋮ menu
- Arrange photos by Newest first, Oldest first, Name A to Z, Name Z to A or Largest first; the name and size sorts show one plain list with no dates (Settings › Photos)

## v0.5.9

- Free up space (in the ⋮ menu): finds exact duplicate photos and videos (keeping the first), videos over 100 MB and screenshots older than a month, with how much each frees. Pick what to clear and it goes to the Trash, so you have 30 days to change your mind
- Fixed a crash when moving around with arrow keys or Tab (a keyboard or remote) while a screen was closing

## v0.5.8

- Lighter on the battery: in the Super look the drifting sparkles settle after a few seconds without a touch (the next tap wakes them), so a screen you're reading no longer redraws 25 times a second

## v0.5.7

- Changing the look in Control Room now restyles this app's widgets straight away, with no refresh or restart

## v0.5.6

- Widgets: choose the title colour in each widget's settings (automatic, white, dark, Aqua blue, Super pink or gold), on top of the background looks

## v0.5.5

- Drag to select: hold a photo, then slide across others to select them all at once; the grid scrolls when you reach the edge
- Widgets can now match your look: new Bliss blue and Super neon backgrounds, and “Match my Wonderdesk look” follows whatever Control Room is set to

## v0.5.4

- Fixed a crash when tapping a thumbnail in the viewer's filmstrip
- Fixes from a full round of stress testing on every screen

## v0.5.3

- Built-in video player: videos play right in the viewer with the same glass controls as Cinema (gel play button, ±10 s, scrubber, speed, loop, mute, fill screen), and pause when you swipe away
- The photo viewer follows your look: glass title bar and action dock in Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss or Super, with a themed background instead of plain black
- Filmstrip of thumbnails under the photo to jump around, and the status and navigation bars hide with the controls
- Swipe a photo up or down to close it; pinch zooms around your fingers, and at the edge of a zoomed photo a swipe goes on to the next one
- Camera apps can open their newest shot in Memories (the thumbnail in the corner of the viewfinder), and Back goes straight back to the camera; over the lock screen only your latest shots are shown
- Pixel Camera only opens Google Photos. With Google Photos turned off, Settings › Open from Pixel Camera makes its thumbnail open Memories instead
- New home-screen shortcuts: hold the Memories icon for Newest photo and Favourites
- On this day: photos from today's date in earlier years at the top of your timeline
- Trash: see everything deleted in the last 30 days, restore it or delete it for good
- Pinch the photo grid to make pictures bigger or smaller, and drag the handle on the right to fly through years of photos by month
- Pull down to refresh, copy a photo to the clipboard from Details, and edit videos in Cinema
- Tap the tab you're already on to glide back to the top of its list
- Switches Android greys out as a “Restricted setting” are now explained: the guide walks you through unlocking them in three clear steps, and keeps showing them until the switch is really on (before, on Android 15 and newer it could hide the only hint)
- New Photo frame widget: On this day, favourites, recent photos or one album, changing every 30 minutes to once a day; tap to open the photo or show the next one, with an optional caption
- Nothing required stays hidden: if Android is blocking something the app needs, a slim banner says what and takes you straight to the switch, and Settings › Permissions and access lists everything with its state

## v0.5.2

- Double-tap zoom animates smoothly and panning stops at the photo's edges
- Smoother everywhere: new screens, tabs and Settings ease in instead of snapping, and buttons, tabs, chips and switches give a light tap you can feel (following your phone's touch-feedback setting)
- Android's predictive back: the back gesture shows a live preview of where you're going
- Super look: tap sparkles now match Bliss Player: soft glowing sparks with trails, drifting rainbow colours and a faint aurora, instead of big solid circles

## v0.5.1

- Thumbnails you've already seen appear instantly when you scroll back, and fast flings no longer pile up work
- Much faster and smoother: the app is now built optimised (the way Play Store apps are), so screens open quicker, scrolling is fluid and the APK is about a quarter of the size
- Screens and lists no longer decode pictures on the main thread, and skip redrawing parts that didn't change

## v0.5.0

- New icon
- The photo grid fits more photos across in landscape
- Works properly in landscape: tabs move to a rail at the side so the content keeps its height, and screens that ran out of room now scroll
- If the app ever crashes it says so next time it opens and offers to send a report (only the error, app version and phone model, with file names and links removed). Turn on automatic sending in Control Room › Wonderdesk
- Lists can no longer crash when two items share a name

## v0.4.0

- Set as wallpaper falls back to other apps if the system cropper isn't available
- If the app ever crashes or freezes, a report goes to Control Room so it can be shared and fixed
- App icon art is larger and fills its tile without being cut off

- App icon redrawn so nothing is cut off: round icons fill the space, the rest sit on an Aqua tile
- Smoother and lighter on the battery: the window background no longer animates, tap effects sleep until you tap, and nothing ticks while the app is in the background
- Opening the app no longer waits on Control Room to read the suite look

## v0.3.0

- Timeline, albums, favourites and videos for every photo on the phone
- Viewer with zoom, slideshow, details and map
