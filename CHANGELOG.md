# Memories changelog

## v0.5.4

- Fixed a crash when tapping a thumbnail in the viewer's filmstrip
- Fixes from a full round of stress testing on every screen

## v0.5.3

- Built-in video player: videos play right in the viewer with the same glass controls as Cinema (gel play button, ±10 s, scrubber, speed, loop, mute, fill screen), and pause when you swipe away
- The photo viewer follows your look: glass title bar and action dock in Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss or Super, with a themed background instead of plain black
- Filmstrip of thumbnails under the photo to jump around, and the status and navigation bars hide with the controls
- Swipe a photo up or down to close it; pinch zooms around your fingers, and at the edge of a zoomed photo a swipe goes on to the next one
- Camera apps can open their newest shot in Memories (the thumbnail in the corner of the viewfinder), and Back goes straight back to the camera; over the lock screen only your latest shots are shown
- Pixel Camera only opens Google Photos. With Google Photos turned off, Settings › Open from Pixel Camera makes its thumbnail open Memories instead
- New home-screen shortcuts: hold the Memories icon for Newest photo and Favourites
- On this day: photos from today's date in earlier years at the top of your timeline
- Trash: see everything deleted in the last 30 days, restore it or delete it for good
- Pinch the photo grid to make pictures bigger or smaller, and drag the handle on the right to fly through years of photos by month
- Pull down to refresh, copy a photo to the clipboard from Details, and edit videos in Cinema
- Tap the tab you're already on to glide back to the top of its list
- Switches Android greys out as a “Restricted setting” are now explained: the guide walks you through unlocking them in three clear steps, and keeps showing them until the switch is really on (before, on Android 15 and newer it could hide the only hint)
- New Photo frame widget: On this day, favourites, recent photos or one album, changing every 30 minutes to once a day; tap to open the photo or show the next one, with an optional caption
- Nothing required stays hidden: if Android is blocking something the app needs, a slim banner says what and takes you straight to the switch, and Settings › Permissions and access lists everything with its state

## v0.5.2

- Double-tap zoom animates smoothly and panning stops at the photo's edges
- Smoother everywhere: new screens, tabs and Settings ease in instead of snapping, and buttons, tabs, chips and switches give a light tap you can feel (following your phone's touch-feedback setting)
- Android's predictive back: the back gesture shows a live preview of where you're going
- Super look: tap sparkles now match Bliss Player: soft glowing sparks with trails, drifting rainbow colours and a faint aurora, instead of big solid circles

## v0.5.1

- Thumbnails you've already seen appear instantly when you scroll back, and fast flings no longer pile up work
- Much faster and smoother: the app is now built optimised (the way Play Store apps are), so screens open quicker, scrolling is fluid and the APK is about a quarter of the size
- Screens and lists no longer decode pictures on the main thread, and skip redrawing parts that didn't change

## v0.5.0

- New icon
- The photo grid fits more photos across in landscape
- Works properly in landscape: tabs move to a rail at the side so the content keeps its height, and screens that ran out of room now scroll
- If the app ever crashes it says so next time it opens and offers to send a report (only the error, app version and phone model, with file names and links removed). Turn on automatic sending in Control Room › Wonderdesk
- Lists can no longer crash when two items share a name

## v0.4.0

- Setting a photo as your wallpaper no longer throws you out of the photo you were looking at
- Set as wallpaper falls back to other apps if the system cropper isn't available
- If the app ever crashes or freezes, a report goes to Control Room so it can be shared and fixed
- App icon art is larger and fills its tile without being cut off

## v0.3.1

- App icon redrawn so nothing is cut off: round icons fill the space, the rest sit on an Aqua tile
- Smoother and lighter on the battery: the window background no longer animates, tap effects sleep until you tap, and nothing ticks while the app is in the background
- Opening the app no longer waits on Control Room to read the suite look

## v0.3.0

- Timeline, albums, favourites and videos for every photo on the phone
- Viewer with zoom, slideshow, details and map
- Editor: crop, rotate, adjust, filters and markup
