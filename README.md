# Memories

All your photos and videos, with editing.

## What it does

- Every photo and video on your phone in a timeline grouped by day, plus **Albums**, **Favourites** and **Videos**. Change the grid size and search by name, album or date.
- Swipe through photos, pinch or double-tap to zoom, and run a slideshow. Details show camera settings, size and location, with a map link.
- Select many to share, favourite, move to an album (or a new one), or delete to Android's recycle bin.
- **Edit**: crop with aspect ratios, rotate and flip, brightness/contrast/saturation/warmth, filters, and drawing markup. Edits are saved as a copy in Pictures/Memories.
- Show any photo in Wayfinder, or set it as your wallpaper.
